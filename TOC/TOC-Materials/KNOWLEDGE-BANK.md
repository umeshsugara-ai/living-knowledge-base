### APRIL

**21st April — Visa Blueprint Part 2: Italy, France, New Zealand**
Speakers: Kshitij Garg (Estero Education, Auckland alumni coordinator), Shagun Handa (Campus France Delhi), Ruby Thomas (Uni-Italia India).
- **New Zealand**: all 8 unis in global QS top 500; India-NZ FTA pending; ~76% recent approval rate, target 27,000 Indian students by 2030. UG needs 75%+ (best-of-4) or IB 24, IELTS 6.0. PG needs first-class + IELTS 6.5, no GRE/GMAT. Costs: UG ₹30-75L total, Masters ₹18-50L. Visa fully online, no interview. 3-yr open Post-Study Work Visa. Only nationalized-bank loans (secured against property) accepted — NBFC/unsecured loans rejected.
- **France**: target 30,000 Indian students by 2030. "Classes Internationales" = 1-yr French-language foundation pathway, capped €5,500. Public tuition ~€2,895/yr. APS post-study stay-back: 2 yrs for Master's grads only.
- **Italy**: 700+ English-taught degrees. 3+2 system. Public fees €300-1,000/yr (income-adjusted). MAECI scholarship = tuition waiver + ~€9,000/yr stipend. Strict 3-tier document legalization (university → state HRD → MEA apostille) — SDM stamps rejected outright.

### MAY

**2nd May — Members Meet** *(not one of the 23 recordings pulled — annual calendar kickoff only)*

**8th May — Funding Dreams: Loans, Scholarships & Financial Aid**
Speakers: Nikhil Jain (ForeignAdmits), Dev Mehta & Payal Chawla (FRR Forex), Shweta Jain (HDFC Credila).
- Banks/portals markup forex 2-2.5%; RBI-authorized Category II dealers (FRR Forex) do ~1% — saves 70-80 paise/USD.
- LRS cap: $250,000/year remittable by resident Indians; only blood relatives (parent/sibling/spouse) can remit for a student — **grandparents cannot** directly.
- TCS: 0% up to ₹7-10L/FY; above that, 0.5% if loan-funded, 2% if self-funded, 5% medical, 20% leisure. Credit against ITR.
- Section 80E: 100% of education-loan interest tax-deductible for 8 years.
- Credila: unsecured loans up to ₹1.5Cr abroad / ₹45-50L India; tenure up to 20 yrs; no foreclosure penalty; part-collateral/hybrid models accepted (agricultural land excluded).

**13th May — College Application Crash Course** *(The College Essay Guy — not one of the 23 pulled recordings)*

**20th May — Telling Your Brand Story Better**

**19th June — Entrance Exams & Pathways in India Part 1: Engineering & Medicine**
Speakers: Anurag Tiwari (Sparkle Edventure/ex-Aakash), Chirag Khutia (ConsultCK). Moderator: Ambika Vasudev.
- **JEE reality**: 12-13 lakh sit JEE Main, but only ~40,000 govt-funded seats (IIT/NIT/IIIT/GFTI) exist — >1.16M rejected annually.
- **NEET reality**: 2.2M appear, 1.23M qualify (50th percentile), only ~58-60K govt MBBS seats (~1:40 ratio). A 30-mark swing = 14,000-18,000 AIR rank swing.
- Tie-break order: Biology → Chemistry → Physics → incorrect:correct ratio.
- AIQ (MCC, 15% of seats + AIIMS/JIPMER/Central Univs) runs concurrently with State counseling (85% + private) — families must register for both.
- Due-diligence checklist for private medical colleges: total 5.5-yr cost, service-bond clauses, cadaver-to-student ratio, WDoMS listing (for USMLE/PLAB eligibility later).
- Branch-vs-college rule of thumb: prioritize branch if going core-technical; prioritize brand if aiming for non-core exits (consulting/MBA).

### JULY

**3rd July — Inside the UC (University of California) System**
Speakers: Aastha Chhikara (UC Riverside), Beauti Deb (UCSC), Vaishnavi Kotiyal (UC Merced).
- 10 campuses, 9 undergrad, 300,000+ students, each campus decides independently.
- Timeline: Aug 1 app opens → Oct 1-Nov 30 filing → March decisions → May 1 SIR deadline.
- **Test-blind**: SAT/ACT not reviewed at all, for admission or scholarships.
- Personal Insight Questions: answer 4 of 8, 350 words max each, **no external links reviewed**.
- Merit scholarships for international/non-resident: UCR up to $89,100/4yr, UCSC up to $100,000/4yr, UC Merced $100,000/4yr (GPA 3.25+). App fee $95 international.

**8th July — Beyond Black Robes: Rethinking Careers in Law**
Speakers: Deepu Krishna (BITS Law School), Harsh Vardhan Tiwari (O.P. Jindal Global Law School).
- Practicing in India → do UG law in India (avoids BCI's mandatory 2-yr bridge course for foreign law degrees). Going abroad only makes sense if practicing there, or as an LL.M. specialization after Indian UG.
- AI (Harvey, Claude, Lucero) automating junior tasks (case summaries, first drafts) — human judgment/advocacy/client trust still irreplaceable.
- 3 law-student archetypes: proactive/career-focused, complacent/brand-reliant, late bloomers (catch up by Yr3).

**15th July — Creative Futures**
Speakers: Emilie Li (SCAD), Sumit Saurabh (Design Circle International).
- SCAD: 85% of career success is soft skills, 15% technical (their claimed stat). 63%+ of SCAD degrees are STEM-designated → 3-yr OPT eligibility. SCADpro = in-house studio pairing students with Fortune 500s (Google, BMW, NASA).
- Portfolio geography differences: **USA** wants polished final execution; **UK/Europe** wants process/sketchbooks; **India govt colleges** (NIFT/NID/UCEED) are pure entrance-exam-driven, private (BITS/FLAME) want holistic portfolios.

### "Convert Consumption into Capability" — adult-learning framework

Source: `Learning-Resources/DOC-20260429-WA0002-.pdf`. A short research-backed piece on why webinar/conference learning doesn't stick (Ebbinghaus forgetting curve: ~70% gone in 24hrs, ~90% in a week) and a **4-stage system**: **Capture** (paraphrase during input, capture friction not content, write questions not answers) → **Return** (retrieval not re-reading, trigger-based not calendar-based) → **Apply** (anchor to existing routine, make it embarrassingly small, find the cue not the willpower) → **Calibrate** (decide depth *before* consuming, not after). Grounded in real citations (Bjork & Bjork desirable-difficulties, Roediger & Karpicke testing effect, Gollwitzer implementation intentions).
Directly reusable for how Vidysea structures any internal training or counselor-facing content.
