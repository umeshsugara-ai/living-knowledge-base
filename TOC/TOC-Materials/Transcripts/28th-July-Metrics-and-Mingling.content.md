## Key Topics & Takeaways

### 1. Navigating Conferences as an Introvert vs. Extrovert
- **The Introvert Experience:** Over 80% of attendees in counseling communities identify as introverts and often feel overwhelmed by large networking halls.
- **Introvert Strategies:**
  - Leverage pre-conference mobile apps to schedule short 15-minute 1-on-1 meetings ahead of time.
  - Utilize a "wingman" or familiar colleague within the community to anchor conversations and boost confidence.
  - Attend speaker sessions and engage speakers with specific questions or feedback immediately afterwards.
  - Remember that listening and observing are valid forms of participation; you do not need to speak with everyone.
- **The Extrovert Perspective & Inclusivity:**
  - Extroverts can practice the "Plate Space" rule: invite solo or passing individuals to join tables/groups during coffee breaks and meals.
  - Structure pitches using the **30-second / 3-minute / 30-minute** framework to respect group time.

### 2. Networking Tactics & Conversation Management
- **Conversation Starters:** Lead with curiosity about the other person (*How to Win Friends and Influence People* principle) rather than an immediate business pitch.
- **Exiting Awkward or Monopolized Conversations:**
  - Use polite exit lines: "I'll let you carry on, great meeting you," or "I need to grab a coffee/take a call."
  - Ask the person rambling to summarize their point or excuse yourself firmly ("I have to run").

### 3. Measuring Return on Investment (ROI) of Events
- **Clarify Event Goals:** Determine whether the goal is institutional brand visibility ("foot in the door"), conversions/partnerships, or professional learning/development.
- **Direct & Indirect Costs:** Account for flight, lodging, transport, and time away from students, not just registration fees.
- **Post-Event Execution:**
  - Block out dedicated calendar time immediately post-conference for follow-ups.
  - Use automated OCR/AI tools (e.g., Claude, mobile scanners) to transcribe business cards directly into spreadsheets.
  - Connect with contacts on LinkedIn or send follow-ups during the event/breaks rather than waiting days later when inboxes are flooded.

### 4. Community Event Formats
- Multi-day retreat/residency formats allow organic networking and lower stress compared to tightly packed one-day events.
- Scheduling must consider major external milestones (e.g., SAT test dates, school terms, major concerts/holidays).
