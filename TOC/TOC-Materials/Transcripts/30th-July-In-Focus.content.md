## Key Topics & Takeaways

### StarterSky (Presented by Anisha Parikh)
- **Mission & Concept:** A 3-to-4 month entrepreneurship accelerator for students aged 13+ (Grades 8–12) designed to guide them from idea generation to product launch, mentorship, competitions, and optional internships.
- **Structure & Format:**
  - *Phase 1 (Idea to Launch):* Problem discovery, competitor analysis, customer validation, prototype/landing page creation, and pitch storytelling (conducted either as a 5-day intensive or flexible 1:1 sessions).
  - *Phase 2 (Mentorship & Competitions):* 1:1 mentorship with founders and academics from IIT, IIM, Harvard, NYU, Stanford, etc., along with support for global student competitions (Diamond Challenge, Conrad Challenge, Blue Ocean).
  - *Phase 3 (Internships & Outcomes):* Optional 2–4 week startup placement, tangible product/service launch, recommendation letters, and personal narrative development for college essays.
- **Pricing & Timing:**
  - Group Track (5-day intensive): ₹75,000 + taxes.
  - Individual Track (flexible 1:1): ₹90,000 + taxes.
  - No technical/coding skills required (focuses on no-code tools and AI-driven platforms).

### New Zealand Higher Education & Estero Education Services (Presented by Kshitij Garg)
- **University Quality & Global Standing:**
  - All 8 state universities in New Zealand rank within the global top 500 (QS Rankings).
  - Ranked #1 English-speaking nation for preparing students for the future by the Economist Intelligence Unit due to practical, hands-on curricula and yearly updated industry-aligned coursework.
- **Visa & Financial Processing Insights:**
  - High historical visa approval rate (~92% average over 15 years; ~85% in recent challenging years).
  - Immigration New Zealand focuses strictly on finances and genuine intent (including acknowledging intention to use the Post-Study Work Visa).
  - Documentation requires exact compliance without omissions.
- **Post-Study Work Visa (PSWV) & Career Prospects:**
  - Bachelor’s and Master’s graduates receive an automatic 3-year Post-Study Work Visa without requiring employer sponsorship upfront.
  - High-demand employment sectors: Construction & Building Sciences (Civil Engineering, Architecture, Quantity Surveying), IT & Computing (Cybersecurity, Data Science, Game Programming, AI Law), Engineering (15 core disciplines), Applied & Life Sciences (Food Science, Sustainability, Biomedical), Accounting & Finance, Healthcare & Nursing.
- **Age Criteria & Study Gaps:**
  - There is no strict age bar for study visas (visas granted to applicants over 50 and 60).
  - Documented work experience or justified life events (sabbaticals, childcare, exam prep) are fully accepted.
  - Permanent residency pathways require applicants to be under 55 years of age at application time.
- **Medical Admissions (MBBS / BDS):**
  - Two medical schools: University of Auckland and University of Otago.
  - Pathway: Entrance into Bachelor of Health Sciences (Year 1), requiring ≥80% (Auckland) or ≥75% (Otago) in Class 12 (or 24–26 IB points). Selection into Year 2 MBBS/BDS is based on Year 1 academic GPA, UCAT exam, and Multi-Mini Interviews (MMI).
  - International quotas: ~8 seats at Auckland and ~15 seats at Otago (with a third medical school scheduled to open around 2027/2028).
