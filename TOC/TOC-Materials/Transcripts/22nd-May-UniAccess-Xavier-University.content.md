## Key Topics & Takeaways

### Mission, Values, and Academic Culture
- **Jesuit Heritage & Cura Personalis:** Xavier emphasizes *cura personalis* ("care for the entire person"), focusing on holistic student development—intellectually, spiritually, and emotionally.
- **Class Sizes & Faculty Engagement:** 
  - Student-to-faculty ratio is **11:1**.
  - Average class size is approximately **20–23 students**.
  - Small class sizes enable faculty mentorship, personalized guidance, and active classroom participation rather than large, anonymous lecture halls.
- **Majors & Flexibility:** Offers **80+ majors and minors** (including STEM, Business, Nursing, Psychology, Sports Management, Neuroscience, and Digital Media), with strong encouragement for double majors and interdisciplinary studies.
- **Research & Recognition:** Awarded a **$2.8 million grant from the NIH**, making Xavier one of only six institutions selected in the inaugural cohort.

### Campus Environment & Student Life
- **Location:** Urban campus situated in a residential neighborhood in Cincinnati, Ohio (2 miles from Downtown Cincinnati, ~19 miles from the Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky International Airport).
- **Corporate Ecosystem:** Proximity to 8 Fortune 500 headquarters (and 150+ Fortune 500 companies in the broader region) provides rich internship and post-graduation employment pathways.
- **Athletics:** Home to **19 NCAA Division I varsity teams** competing in the BIG EAST Conference, centered around game-day culture at the Cintas Center.
- **First-Year Transition:** Programs such as *Move Crew* and *Manresa Orientation* ensure new students (cohort of ~1,650 first-year students) build immediate social and academic support networks.

### Application Process & Financial Aid
- **Application Deadlines:**
  - *November 1:* Early Action application deadline (December 1 notification).
  - *February 1:* Regular Decision application deadline (Early March notification).
  - *After March:* Rolling admission decisions.
- **Application Requirements:**
  - Common Application or Xavier Online Application.
  - High school transcripts (Grades 9–12 / predicted scores).
  - One letter of recommendation (counselor, teacher, or mentor; independent counselor recommendations accepted).
  - Personal essay.
  - Optional standardized test scores (SAT/ACT).
  - English language proficiency test scores.
- **Scholarships & Affordability:**
  - **100%** of admitted international families receive a scholarship opportunity.
  - Merit scholarships range from **$23,000 to $36,000 annually**.
  - Additional awards include the Muskie Grant (up to $3,000/year) and need-based/engagement-based Financial Aid (up to $5,000/year).
  - Effective annual tuition after aid can be as low as **$11,500**.
  - Participating in virtual sessions and meeting admissions representatives can open doors for additional financial assistance.
