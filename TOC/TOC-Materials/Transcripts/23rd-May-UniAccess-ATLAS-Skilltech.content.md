## Summary
This session features an institutional presentation by ATLAS SkillTech University located in Mumbai, India. The presenter highlights the university's multidisciplinary schools, international academic collaborations with US institutions like Parsons and Babson, diverse degree offerings, and placement ecosystem. Additionally, information regarding student clubs, campus housing options, and summer school programs for prospective students is discussed.

## Speakers / Panelists
* **Farheen** – Representative, ATLAS SkillTech University
* **TOC Representative / Moderator** – Host from The Outreach Collective

## Organizations & Programs Mentioned
* **ATLAS SkillTech University** (situated at BKC, Kurla West, Mumbai)
* **ISDI (School of Design & Innovation)** (in collaboration with Parsons School of Design)
* **ISME (School of Management & Entrepreneurship)** (affiliated/collaborating with Babson College group)
* **uGDX (School of Technology / Digital Technology)**
* **School of Law** (Bar Council of India approved)
* **upGrad Living** (On-campus accommodation partner)
* **Summer Programs**: Design Summer School, Tech Summer School, ISME Summer School

## Key Topics & Takeaways
* **Campus & Location**:
  * Located centrally at the Bandra-Kurla Complex (BKC) / Kurla West in Mumbai, providing close proximity to corporate hubs.
  * Campus infrastructure consists of two main buildings (7-storey and 11-storey) housing design studios, modern classrooms, and hostel facilities via upGrad Living.
* **Schools & Academic Structure**:
  * **ISDI (Design & Innovation)**: Offers B.Des specializations in Fashion Communication, Product Design, Fashion Design, Interior Design, and Strategic Design & Management.
  * **ISME (Management & Entrepreneurship)**: Offers BBA degrees with various majors/minors including Psychology, Finance, Digital Branding & Advertising, and AI/Emerging Technologies; also features an International Accounting and Finance pathway with ACCA/paper exemptions.
  * **uGDX (Technology)**: Offers B.Tech degrees focusing on CS, AI & ML, Robotics, Data Science, and Cybersecurity.
  * **School of Law**: Offers a 5-year integrated BBA LL.B. (Hons.) program.
* **Experiential Learning & Placements**:
  * Mandatory startup creation/ideation requirement for business students by the third year.
  * Structured internship cycles every semester (short winter internships and long summer internships).
  * Robust placement support across major multinational corporations, consulting firms, technology giants, and marketing agencies.
* **Student Life & Enrichment**:
  * Vibrant student clubs covering performing arts (STAGE), sports (AGILE), finance (FINTECEST), entrepreneurship (Futurepreneurs), and community service (National Service Scheme).
  * High school summer programs offered across Design, Management, and Technology tracks.
