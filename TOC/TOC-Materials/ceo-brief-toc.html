01What TOC actually is

TOC is not a product or technology company — it's a paid membership community for India's education-services sector: school counsellors, Independent Education Counsellors (IECs), university admissions teams, and ed-service founders. Its asset is four years of accumulated trust between senior decision-makers who show up to the same room every month. That isn't a feature we can code around.

05The actual decision in front of us

This is not "build vs. buy a software feature." It's "build vs. join a distribution channel." Three real options:

Option A

Stay a passive member (status quo)

Cost: whatever the membership fee is (not disclosed in materials reviewed — get this from Karunn). Benefit: attendance access to counsellors/IECs, but no active promotion of Pathlynks to that room.

Option B — Recommended

Apply to be featured on "In Focus"

The exact mechanism RECO Education and Education & You used in August — TOC picked them through a review process and gave them a 45-min slot in front of 357 senior counsellors/IECs/admissions teams, plus pre/post promotion. Applications reportedly open annually. Cheapest, fastest way to reach exactly the audience Pathlynks needs, using infrastructure TOC already built.

Option C — Not recommended

Build a Vidysea-run community from scratch

What it would take, per TOC's own trajectory: four years, a majority-referral acquisition motion (paid marketing alone won't replicate 70% peer-referral trust), and enough independent value that members pay themselves rather than expecting it free from a vendor. A vendor-run "community" built to sell Pathlynks reads as a webinar funnel, not a peer association — TOC members explicitly renew for learning, not vendor access. Hard to fake from inside a company with a product to sell.
