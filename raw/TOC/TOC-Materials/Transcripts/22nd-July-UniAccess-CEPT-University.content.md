## Key Topics & Takeaways

### Academic Philosophy & Pedagogy
* **Foundation & Structure:** Founded in 1962 by Pritzker Prize-winning architect B.V. Doshi. CEPT has grown from a School of Architecture to include Planning, Technology, Design, and Management.
* **Studio-Based Learning:** 70% of the academic credits come from studio work (intensive active making in cohorts of 10 to 12 students tackling real-world problems).
* **Progression Structure:**
  * **L1 (Foundation):** CEPT Foundation Program (CFP) common first year across all disciplines focusing on drawing, drafting, making, and critical thinking.
  * **L2 (Core Abilities):** Vertical studios combining 2nd and 3rd-year students with cross-disciplinary electives.
  * **L3 (Synthesis):** 4th and 5th-year students tackling multi-layered challenges.
  * **L4 (Mastery):** Postgraduate and specialized master's level research/practice.
* **Choice-Based Curriculum:** 30% of the syllabus is fixed; the remaining 70% is choice-based, allowing students to select from ~100 studios each semester across different disciplines.

### Admissions Criteria & Metrics
* **B.Arch (120 seats):** 50% Board exam aggregate + 50% NATA score. Requires 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics (Chemistry is no longer mandatory under COA norms).
* **BUD / Bachelor of Urban Design (40 seats):** 50% Board aggregate + 50% NATA or JEE Main Paper 2 score.
* **B.Des (100 seats):** Purely based on valid UCEED scores (Part A & B). Open to students from any high school academic stream (no science/math requirement). All 100 seats are in the open/general merit category.
* **Civil Engineering (40 seats):** Admissions based on JEE Main Paper 1 (All India) or GUJCET (ACPC quota).
* **Portfolios:** Portfolios are **not** required for undergraduate admission at CEPT (selection is purely score/merit-based via NATA/UCEED/JEE and board exams).

### Student Life, Exhibitions, and Global Engagement
* **Exhibitions & Juries:** Twice a year (December and May), CEPT holds public campus exhibitions of all studio output. External juries evaluate studio work and award CEPT Excellence Awards.
* **Summer & Winter Schools (SWS):** Over 100 intensive short-term courses involving national and international travel or campus masterclasses.
* **International Collaboration:** Active exchange programs and joint studios with ~40 international partner institutions.
* **Post-Graduation Pathways:** ~20% of undergraduate alumni pursue master's degrees abroad at top international design/architecture schools. Many graduates start their own specialized practices, work with top firms, or join corporate consultancies and governmental urban planning bodies.
