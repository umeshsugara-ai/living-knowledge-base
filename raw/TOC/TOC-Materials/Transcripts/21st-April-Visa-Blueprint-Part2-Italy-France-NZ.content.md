## Summary

Representatives from educational consulting and official cultural/governmental agencies share detailed step-by-step guidance on application processes, documentation authentication, financial requirements, and common pitfalls to avoid during visa applications.

## Key Topics & Takeaways

### 1. Studying in New Zealand (Kshitij Garg)
* **General Landscape & Diplomatic Context:**
  * All 8 New Zealand universities rank in the top 500 globally (QS 2026).
  * India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement (FTA) is moving toward signing, boosting bilateral educational and business ties.
  * Over 8,000 Indian student applications approved recently (~76% approval rate); target is 27,000 by 2030.
* **Admissions Requirements:**
  * *Undergraduate:* Minimum 75% in best of 4 subjects (CBSE/ICSE/State boards) or 24 points in IB Diploma. IELTS 6.0 (no band < 5.5) or equivalent (PTE/TOEFL/Duolingo). University of Auckland requires 80%+ and does not accept Duolingo. English waiver available if scoring 60%+ in standard 12 English.
  * *Postgraduate:* Minimum first-class bachelor's or CGPA ≥ 6.3. IELTS 6.5 (no band < 6.0). Backlogs accepted primarily if in Years 1–2. No GRE/GMAT required.
* **Costs & Scholarships:**
  * Tuition: Master's degrees range from INR 18–50 Lacs; Bachelor's degrees range from INR 30–75 Lacs (total 3-year cost).
  * Living Costs: Mandated by INZ at NZD 20,000/year (~INR 11 Lacs).
  * Scholarships: Guaranteed scholarships up to NZD 10,000–15,000; Merit-based scholarships up to NZD 20,000–50,000.
* **Visa & Residency Pathways:**
  * Entire visa process is online (e-Visa; no physical passport submission or mandatory biometric interviews).
  * 3-year open Post-Study Work Visa granted after eligible Bachelor's or Master's degrees.
  * Work rights: 25 hours/week during term; full-time during holidays.
  * Residence Pathway: Graduates earning the median wage (~NZD 72,000/year) can apply for Resident Visa directly (Master's) or after 1 year of work (Bachelor's from August 2026). Permanent Residency after 2 years of residence; Citizenship eligible after 5 years total.
* **Financial Requirements:**
  * Must show funds covering tuition + living expenses for the entire program duration.
  * Accepted funds: Education loans from 14 approved nationalized banks (secured against property only; NBFCs and unsecured loans not accepted), savings accounts (3-month vintage; bulk deposits need proof of source), fixed deposits (minimum 6 months old), and withdrawable Provident Fund balances.
  * Eligible sponsors: Parents, real siblings, maternal/paternal grandparents only.

### 2. Studying in France (Shagun Handa)
* **Overview & Goals:**
  * Objective to host 30,000 Indian students in France by 2030.
  * Over 3,500 institutions, 204 engineering schools, and 45 management schools.
* **Classes Internationales Program:**
  * A 1-year foundational/pathway program for Indian high school graduates to learn French (up to B2 level) directly at their chosen French higher education institution before transitioning into a French-taught Bachelor's program.
  * Free application directly via the official portal; capped tuition at €5,500 for the foundational year.
* **Costs & Scholarships:**
  * Public university tuition: ~€2,895/year for undergraduate programs. Private institutions range up to €15,000+/year.
  * Living cost estimates: ~€1,100/month in Paris; ~€800/month in other cities (Lyon, Toulouse, Nice, etc.).
  * Government scholarships include France Excellence Charpak and France Excellence Eiffel (merit-based only).
* **Admissions & Visa Process:**
  * Two routes: Direct application to school followed by pre-consular Campus France (EEF) process, or applying to up to 3 undergraduate / 7 postgraduate programs directly through the EEF portal (October–January window).
  * Mandatory academic interview with Campus France manager.
  * Visa Fee: Subsidized rate of €50 for international students (free for scholarship holders).
* **Financial & Accommodation Guidelines:**
  * Funds required: €850/month for living expenses + 1 year of tuition fee (shown via savings, FDs, or loan sanction letters).
  * Only parents/siblings can sponsor (or third-party with an official affidavit).
  * Accommodation proof must be confirmed for at least 3 weeks to 1 month (dummy/unconfirmed bookings lead to rejection).
* **Post-Study Work Options:**
  * APS (*Autorisation Provisoire de Séjour*): 2-year non-renewable stay-back (1 year + 1 year renewal) available exclusively for Indian Master's graduates under a bilateral agreement.
  * RECE Visa: Available for graduates applying from India within 4 years of obtaining their degree.

### 3. Studying in Italy (Ruby Thomas)
* **Overview & Educational Framework:**
  * Follows the European 3+2 system (3-year Bachelor's *Laurea Triennale*, 2-year Master's *Laurea Magistrale*, 3-year PhD *Dottorato*).
  * Offers over 700+ English-taught degree courses across public and private universities.
* **Admissions, Costs & Scholarships:**
  * Application cycle starts nearly a year in advance (October–January for September intake).
  * Public university fees range between €300–€1,000/year (often adjusted based on family income documentation). Private universities range from €10,000–€12,000/year.
  * Living costs: €1,000/month in cities like Milan/Rome; €300–€400/month in university towns like Padua/Pavia.
  * Scholarships: MAECI (Ministry of Foreign Affairs) provides tuition waiver + ~€9,000/year stipend; Invest Your Talent in Italy; regional income/need-based scholarships.
* **Crucial Visa & Document Legalization Steps:**
  * **Academic vs. Residential Jurisdiction:** Visa and legalization must align strictly with consular jurisdiction (based on last education completed or residence of the last 6+ months).
  * **Pre-Enrollment:** Mandatory submission via the *Universitaly* portal for one chosen university. Once submitted, it cannot be modified.
  * **Attestation & Apostille:** 3-tier mandatory process: University verification stamp -> State HRD/Higher Education Department verification -> MEA Apostille. SDM (Sub-Divisional Magistrate) stamps are strictly rejected.
  * **DOV (*Dichiarazione di Valore*) / CIMEA:** Official document verifying the validity of Indian academic credentials.
* **Financial Requirements:**
  * Minimum €7,000/year living expense requirement shown in recurring transactional accounts or education loans disbursed into the account.
  * Only parents or the applicant themselves can act as sponsors.
  * Visa issued for 1 year; students must apply for the *Permesso di Soggiorno* (Residence Permit) within 8 days of arriving in Italy.
* **Post-Study Work Options:**
  * 1-year stay-back permit for job seeking (*Permesso di Soggiorno per ricerca lavoro*) for Master's and PhD graduates, convertible to a full work permit upon securing employment.
