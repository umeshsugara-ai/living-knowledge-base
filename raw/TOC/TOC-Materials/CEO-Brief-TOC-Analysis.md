# CEO Brief: The Outreach Collective (TOC)

## What it is, what membership actually delivers, and whether Vidysea should build this in-house

---

## Recommendation

**Don't build a competing community. Stay a member and use it far more aggressively than we currently are.**

TOC is not a product or a technology company — it is a **membership-based professional community** for India's education-services sector (school counsellors, Independent Education Counsellors, university admissions teams, and ed-service founders). Its asset is four years of accumulated trust and 357 senior decision-makers who show up in the same room every month. That is not a feature set we can code around — it's a network-effect asset that took TOC four years, a specific acquisition motion (peer referral, not paid marketing), and first-mover positioning to build. Replicating it would cost more in time than in money, and the market may not support two such communities.

Vidysea/Pathlynks is currently a **passive member** — Karunn was invited to attend "In Focus" as an audience member. We are not yet a *featured* company (the way RECO Education and Education & You were in August). That's the actual gap worth closing, not TOC's existence.

## What TOC Actually Is (in plain business terms)

**Business model:** Annual paid membership (fee amount not disclosed in materials reviewed) into a curated professional community. 70% of members self-pay out of pocket; organisational sponsorship is growing fast (44 → 109 sponsored memberships year-on-year, a 2.5× jump) — early signal that institutions are starting to fund what individuals were already paying for themselves.

**Scale (2026–27 cohort, per TOC's own "Anatomy of a Professional Community" report):**
- 357 members across 332 organisations, 13 countries beyond India
- 74% year-on-year renewal rate; 70% joined via peer/colleague referral (not paid marketing)
- 3.6× faster member acquisition than Year One — 155 new members/month this cycle vs. 43/month in 2023-24
- 75% of new members chose TOC as their *only* professional membership (not stacking associations)
- Median member age 43; 64% have 10+ years of experience — this is a senior, credentialed room

**The product members actually get** — 56+ "experiences" across 11 months (April 2026–Feb 2027), 84% virtual/free-with-membership, a handful premium and paid:

| Format | What it is | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| **In Focus** | Monthly 45-min showcase — 2 curated service-provider companies present + live demo + Q&A to the full membership. TOC handles pre-event promotion, a prep call, recordings, and facilitated follow-up connections. | Last Thursday, monthly |
| **UniAccess Live** | Back-to-back virtual "fairs" — universities present directly to members (Intl + India editions) | ~2x/year |
| **Topical webinars** | Visa compliance, UCAS/admissions changes, entrance exams, career-pathway deep dives (law, healthcare, sports, diplomacy...), AI/business skills for IECs | ~2-4/month |
| **Premium in-person** (paid separately) | The Residency (AI summer school), Sakura Immersion (6-day Japan trip w/ university access), The Soiree (networking gala), The Conclave (72-hr founder fellowship, 10 seats), Fourth Weave (flagship annual conference) | 6 events/year |

The 23 session recordings we pulled and transcribed (April–August 2026) confirm this in practice: real panels with named university reps (Ashoka, Xavier Cincinnati, ATLAS SkillTech, CEPT, Durham, Southampton, Warwick, Exeter, QMUL), real practitioners (visa agencies, career coaches, a SCAD admissions director, a design-portfolio consultant), not vendor-pitch filler.

## The Actual Decision In Front Of Us

This is **not** "build vs. buy a software feature." It's **"build vs. join a distribution channel."** Three real options:

### Option A — Stay a passive member (status quo)
Cost: whatever the membership fee is (get this from Karunn/finance — not in the materials reviewed). Benefit: attendance access to counsellors/IECs, but no active promotion of Vidysea/Pathlynks to that room.

### Option B — Apply to be featured on "In Focus" (recommended first move)
This is the exact mechanism RECO Education and Education & You used in August — TOC picked them through a review process and gave them a 45-min slot in front of 357 senior counsellors/IECs/admissions teams, plus pre/post promotion. Applications reportedly open annually (per the programming doc, "Applications to participate Open April 12th" for the 2026-27 cycle — worth checking if a window is still open or when the next opens). This is the cheapest, fastest way to get Pathlynks in front of exactly the audience it needs, using infrastructure TOC already built.

### Option C — Build a Vidysea-run community from scratch
What it would take, based on TOC's own trajectory: **4 years**, a majority-referral acquisition motion (paid marketing alone won't replicate 70% peer-referral trust), and enough independent value (56+ curated sessions/year, real content) that members pay *themselves* rather than expecting it free from a vendor. A vendor-run "community" that exists to sell Pathlynks will read as a webinar funnel, not a peer association — TOC's members explicitly said they renew for *learning*, not vendor access. That's a hard model to fake from inside a company with a product to sell.

**Verdict: Option C is not worth pursuing.** The moat here is trust and time, not technology. Option B is close to free (relative to building) and directly testable within one budget cycle.
