### **Overview & Purpose**
The monthly TOC ‘In Focus’ session introduced counselors and community members to specialized academic partners. The session highlighted collaborative opportunities to support students across test preparation and high-level academic readiness for university transitions.

---

### **Featured Organizations & Details**

#### **1. Education and You (Priyamvada)**
* **Focus:** Niche, 1-on-1 live test preparation for global and Indian entrance exams, including SAT, GRE, GMAT, IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, IIMAT, Bocconi UG, SMU PG, ISB, and various Indian undergraduate entrance tests.
* **Key Numbers & Methodology:** 
  * Has taught over **6,000 students**.
  * Employs diagnostic testing before building personalized study plans.
  * Utilizes verified third-party materials (e.g., College Board, Kaplan, Princeton Review) rather than white-labeled content.
  * Uses a shared Excel tracking sheet accessible to teachers, students, parents, and counselors, alongside mandatory fortnightly PTMs.
  * Provides a proprietary **1,500+ word flashcard box** linked to online quizzes via QR codes.
  * Requires a mandatory 30-minute demo class.
* **Program Timelines & Pricing:** Typically runs as a **3-month program** (10–12 classes per month) or intensive crash courses (25–30 classes). Specific pricing and referral terms are handled on an individual partner basis.

#### **2. Reco Education (Paritosh)**
* **Focus:** 1-on-1 mentorship for students in **Grades 8–12** centered on academic reading, writing, and critical thinking—primarily across humanities, social sciences, economics, psychology, and select STEM-social science intersections.
* **Key Numbers & Methodology:** 
  * Designed to bridge the gap between college access and college success, targeting the academic shock students face with undergraduate-level texts and course selection.
  * Mentors are predominantly PhD candidates (e.g., from Ashoka University).
  * Students produce tangible writing outputs (e.g., 7-essay series published on platforms like Substack) and participate in student showcase presentations (no external academic journal publishing is offered).
  * Leverages AI tools like Google’s NotebookLM for reading comprehension.
* **Program Structure & Pricing:** Standard modules run for a minimum of **9 sessions** (or **12 sessions** for larger capstone projects). Historical pricing is approximately **₹8,000 per session** (all-inclusive). Enrolment is currently closed for the current intake and reopens in the summer.

---

### **Key Takeaway**
Both organizations emphasize high-touch, customized, one-on-one student support, transparent communication with referring counselors, and distinct non-negotiables to build long-term academic capability and admissions readiness.
