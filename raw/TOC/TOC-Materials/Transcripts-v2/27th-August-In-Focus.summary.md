**Overview & Purpose**
This TOC 'In Focus' session, hosted by Devesh and Bhakti, showcased two featured partner organizations—**Education N You** and **Reco Education**. The session aimed to inform educational counselors and community members about specialized test-preparation models and academic reading/writing programs designed to enhance students' college access and long-term academic success.

---

### **Featured Presenters & Offerings**

#### **1. Priyambada (Founder & Chief Mentor, Education N You)**
* **Core Offerings:** Diagnostic-led, customized 1-on-1 live test preparation for global standardized exams (SAT, GRE, GMAT, IELTS, TOEFL, PTE), specialized institutional tests (IE Madrid, Bocconi, SMU PG, ISB BAT), and Indian UG entrance exams.
* **Key Details & Metrics:**
  * **Reach:** Taught over 6,000 students.
  * **Methodology & Non-Negotiables:** Starts with a diagnostic test and a mandatory 30-minute live demo. Offers strictly 1-on-1 live classes (no recorded sessions) with mandatory cameras on.
  * **Tracking & Tools:** Utilizes a shared Excel "Student-Teacher Tracker" visible to parents and counselors, holds fortnightly PTMs, and provides a physical 1,500+ word flashcard box ("mithai ka dabba") linked via QR code to exclusive online games/quizzes.
  * **Structure & Pricing:** Typical preparation spans 3 months (or crash courses of 25–30 classes). Portal practice access remains valid for 1 year. Pricing/referrals are customized per associate counselor upon direct consultation.

#### **2. Parikshit (Founder, Reco Education)**
* **Core Offerings:** Customized, 1-on-1 online courses in academic reading, writing, and critical thinking for students in grades 8–12 (focused on humanities, social sciences, economics, psychology, and multidisciplinary STEM). The program aims to bridge the gap between high school preparation and college-level academic rigor.
* **Key Details & Metrics:**
  * **Structure:** Courses range from 9 sessions (minimum) to 12 sessions (for larger capstone projects).
  * **Pricing:** Approximately ₹8,000 per session, covering curriculum curation, reading materials, mentor feedback, and administrative interface.
  * **Deliverables:** Curated reading lists paired with PhD-level mentors. Students write polished academic essays (e.g., 7 essays totaling 7,500+ words) published independently on platforms like Substack or Medium, followed by live presentation showcases.
  * **Tech Integration:** Encourages AI utilization, specifically leveraging Google's NotebookLM for text comprehension.

---

### **Key Takeaway**
The session emphasized that holistic student preparation requires a dual approach: personalized, transparent test preparation to secure university admission (Education N You), alongside high-level critical reading and writing instruction to prevent academic drifting once enrolled in college (Reco Education).
